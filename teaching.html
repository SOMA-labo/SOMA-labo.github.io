Supervision

We host Master's and rotation projects most terms. Projects tend to be either analytical (a model and its consequences), computational (a question asked of a large sequencing dataset), or experimental, and most end up as some mixture. If you are a student at Université d'Évry or elsewhere in Paris-Saclay looking for a project, write with a sentence or two about what you have enjoyed so far.
