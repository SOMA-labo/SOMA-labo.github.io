Most microbial cells, most of the time, are not growing. Sporulation as a *Bacillota*
life-history strategy produces a seed bank that buffers populations against
deleterious conditions. This reservoir of genetic and phenotypic diversity dampens evolution and its efficacy is determined by cell bioenergetics.
