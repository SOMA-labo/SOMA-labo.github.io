title: Strains, sojourns and evolution inside a host
---
The human gut is an evolution experiment nobody designed, running
in billions of replicates. Dense time series let us watch strains
rise and fall, ask how long a lineage persists once it arrives, and
test whether ecological stability at the species level is really
stability at the level of strains. The same macroecological language
applies, one level down.

Related work:
[mSystems 2026](https://doi.org/10.1128/msystems.01221-25),
[mBio 2023](https://doi.org/10.1128/mbio.02502-22),
[GBE 2022](https://doi.org/10.1093/gbe/evab116).
