Join the lab

We are looking for people who want to move between theory, computation and experiment rather than pick one. You do not need to have done all three — you need to want to.

What it is like

Work here is quantitative. Most projects involve a model you can write down, data large enough that you will write code to handle it, and an argument about whether the two agree. People come from physics, mathematics, computer science, ecology and microbiology; nobody arrives knowing all of it, and part of the job is teaching each other.

We share code and data with our papers, we write things down as we go, and we would rather have a negative result we believe than a positive one we do not. Everyone is welcome here regardless of background, and we mean that in the practical sense of adjusting how we work so that it is true.
