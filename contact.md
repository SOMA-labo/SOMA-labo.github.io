## Enquiries

**Prospective students and postdocs:** please read
[Join](join.html) first — it says what to put in the email,
which makes it much easier to give you a useful answer.

about the audience and the date helps.

**Questions about a paper, its data or its code:** open an
issue on the relevant [GitHub
repository](https://github.com/SOMA-labo) if there is one, so the answer is visible to the next
person who asks.
