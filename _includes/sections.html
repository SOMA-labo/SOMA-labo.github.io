{%- comment -%} Turns a page's rendered Markdown into the site's labelled rows. Every "## Heading" in a page starts a new section. The heading becomes the small label in the left column, and everything up to the next "## Heading" becomes the content on the right:

Heading

...content...
The first block on a page gets the tight spacing (band--tight); every later one gets a hairline rule above it (band--rule). Anything before the first "## Heading" is output as it is.
